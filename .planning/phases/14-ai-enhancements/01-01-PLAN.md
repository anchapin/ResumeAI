# Phase Plan: v1.4 Phase 1 - Smart Writing Assistant

**Version:** 1.0
**Created:** 2026-03-13
**Status:** Ready for Execution

---

## Phase Overview

**Goal:** Provide real-time AI-powered writing suggestions as users edit their resume.

**Requirements:**
- **AI-01:** Smart writing assistant with real-time suggestions
- **AI-02:** Grammar and style checking for resume content
- **AI-03:** Action verb and tone optimization

**Success Criteria:**
1. Real-time suggestions appear as user types (debounced 300-500ms)
2. Grammar and spelling corrections highlighted with wavy underlines
3. Style improvements (stronger verbs, clearer phrasing)
4. Tone optimization (professional, impactful)
5. One-click accept/reject for suggestions
6. Suggestion history and undo support

**Estimated Effort:** 6-8 weeks (4-5 days backend, 4-5 days frontend)

---
